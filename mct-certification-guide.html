🎓 Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) Program Scope

The Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) credential is the premier technical training certification awarded by Microsoft. MCTs are certified subject matter experts validated not only in technical domain excellence but also in instructional pedagogy and adult learning facilitation.

1. Qualification & Annual Renewal Matrix

Requirement Phase Mandatory Deliverable Validation Mechanism
1. Technical Excellence Hold at least one qualifying premier role-based or specialty certification. Azure Solutions Architect, DevOps Expert, Data Engineer, Power Platform Developer, or Business Central Specialist.
2. Instructional Pedagogy Demonstrate verified instructional skills through an accredited trainer program. CompTIA CTT+, Fourth Dimension Technologies, Friesen, Kaye and Associates, or accredited university teaching credentials.
3. Annual Renewal Maintain active teaching status and top student evaluation scores. Deliver Microsoft Official Courseware (MOC) verified through Metrics That Matter (MTM) with an average satisfaction rating ≥ 4.0 out of 5.0.

🧠 Modern Technical Pedagogy: Adult Learning (Andragogy)

Teaching working software architects and systems engineers requires moving beyond rote memorization. MCTs apply Knowles' Principles of Andragogy:

  • Self-Directed Learning: Experienced adult learners want to steer their problem-solving trajectories. Allow students to explore architectural trade-offs rather than dictating single paths.
  • Immediate Problem Relevance: Adults engage deeply when technical concepts immediately solve real business pain points in their daily projects.
  • Experience-Based Framing: Relate new cloud concepts to existing legacy knowledge (e.g., explaining Dataverse tables by comparing them to SQL Server tables, or comparing Docker containers to lightweight virtual machines).
