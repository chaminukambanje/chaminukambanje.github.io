📦 Product Information Management (PIM) & Inventory Dimensions

Products in Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management are structured into Shared Products (global definitions) and Released Products (company-specific operational entities).

1. Dimension Groups Architecture

Product Dimensions

Differentiates physical variants of a product master:

  • Configuration: Custom build combinations.
  • Size: Small, Medium, Large, XL.
  • Color: Red, Blue, Black.
  • Style: Casual, Executive, Heavy-Duty.
  • Version: Hardware revision (v1.0, v2.1).

Storage Dimensions

Governs physical storage location hierarchy:

  • Site: Geographic facility (Mandatory).
  • Warehouse: Physical building under a Site.
  • Location: Specific bin, shelf, or bay.
  • Inventory Status: Available, Damaged, Blocked.
  • License Plate: Pallet/tote identifier.

Tracking Dimensions

Enforces regulatory batch & serial traceability:

  • Batch Number: Tracks production lots with expiration, shelf life, and quarantine dates.
  • Serial Number: Tracks discrete physical serialized units (Active in inventory vs only during sales issue).

2. Item Model Groups & Inventory Costing

Item Model Groups control how transactions post financially to the General Ledger and how cost prices are calculated:

Costing Method Calculation Mechanics Business Application
FIFO (First In, First Out) The earliest received inventory costs are settled first against sales issues. Consumer goods, retail, standard manufacturing with perishable stock.
LIFO (Last In, First Out) The most recently acquired inventory costs are settled first. Permitted in US GAAP; prohibited under international IFRS standards.
Standard Cost Predefined fixed cost per item; variances post to Purchase Price Variance (PPV) accounts. High-volume discrete manufacturing, automotive, electronics assembly.
Weighted Average Calculates an average unit cost weighted by inventory quantities received. Commodities, raw materials, bulk liquids, chemical processing.

⏱️ Master Planning & Planning Optimization (MRP)

The Planning Optimization Add-in is a dedicated cloud microservice running in Azure that generates Material Requirements Planning (MRP) outputs in minutes rather than overnight batch runs.

Static vs Dynamic Master Plans: A Static Plan remains fixed during operational shifts and is used by purchasing teams to firm planned purchase orders. A Dynamic Plan automatically updates whenever unplanned sales orders or inventory adjustments occur, giving real-time visibility into stock availability.

📝 Realistic MB-330 Practice Scenarios

Scenario 1: Three-Way Matching Failure in Procurement

A procurement clerk receives a vendor invoice for 100 industrial pumps at £1,200 per unit. The original Purchase Order was confirmed for 100 pumps at £1,000 per unit. The system blocks posting with a matching variance error.

Question: Which three documents are compared during Dataverse/D365 Three-Way Invoice Matching?

  • A) Purchase Order lines, Product Receipt lines, and Vendor Invoice lines
  • B) Sales Order lines, Picking List lines, and Customer Invoice lines
  • C) Bill of Materials, Route operations, and Production Order lines
  • D) Vendor Quotation, Purchase Requisition, and Bank Statement
Click to view Answer & Detailed Technical Rationale
Correct Answer: A (Purchase Order lines, Product Receipt lines, and Vendor Invoice lines)

Detailed Analysis:

  • Option A is CORRECT: Three-Way Matching validates: 1) The pricing and quantity on the approved Purchase Order, 2) The physical quantity delivered on the warehouse Product Receipt, and 3) The final price and quantities billed on the Vendor Invoice. If the price variance exceeds the company's price tolerance percentage, the invoice is blocked until approved by authorized management.
  • Option B is INCORRECT: These are customer-facing outbound sales documents.
  • Option C is INCORRECT: These are shop-floor manufacturing production entities.
  • Option D is INCORRECT: Bank statements reconcile cash, not procurement line item variances.
