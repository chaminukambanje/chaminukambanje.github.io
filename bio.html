👤 Executive Biography

Chaminuka Mbanje is a versatile, veteran ICT leader, High-Performance Computing (HPC) storage specialist, and Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) with over 25 years of distinguished experience across scientific data infrastructure, enterprise cloud solutions, software engineering, and higher education.

Chaminuka’s unique career trajectory blends deep pedagogical roots with cutting-edge infrastructure engineering. Beginning as a dedicated mathematics educator, he transitioned through higher education roles as a Senior Systems Engineer, Software Engineer, and University ICT Manager, later directing enterprise cloud consulting and technical training programs internationally.
