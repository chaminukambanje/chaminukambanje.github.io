MB-910: Dynamics 365 Fundamentals (CRM)

Complete study guide covering Customer Engagement: Marketing, Sales, Customer Service, Field Service, and Power Platform architecture.

Exam MB-910Dynamics 365CRMSales & MarketingCustomer & Field Service

📘 Exam Overview & Scope

Exam MB-910: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Fundamentals (CRM) validates foundational knowledge of customer engagement capabilities across Dynamics 365 Marketing, Sales, Customer Service, and Field Service, along with cross-application features and Power Platform integration.

Domain Weight Core Key Areas
Describe Dynamics 365 Marketing 15–20% Customer Insights - Journeys, Segments, Lead Scoring, Event Management
Describe Dynamics 365 Sales 15–20% Leads, Opportunities, Quotes, Orders, Invoices, Sales Pipelines, Forecasting
Describe Dynamics 365 Customer Service 15–20% Cases, Knowledge Base, Queues, SLAs, Entitlements, Omnichannel
Describe Dynamics 365 Field Service 15–20% Work Orders, Schedule Board, Universal Resource Scheduling, Connected Field Service
Shared Features & Power Platform 20–25% Dataverse tables, Power Automate, Microsoft Teams & Outlook integration, Copilot

🎯 Module 1: Dynamics 365 Marketing

Dynamics 365 Marketing (now integrated with Customer Insights - Journeys) focuses on identifying, nurturing, and converting prospects.

Customer Journeys & Triggers

Automated visual workflows triggered by user actions (e.g., website visit, email click) or scheduled intervals to send targeted communications.

  • Real-Time Journeys: Responds instantaneously to customer actions.
  • Outbound Marketing: Batch-oriented campaign journeys.

Segments & Lead Scoring

Audience targeting and prioritization based on demographic and behavioral data.

  • Behavioral Segments: Customers who clicked a specific campaign link.
  • Lead Scoring Models: Adds points for engagement, decaying inactive leads.

💼 Module 2: Dynamics 365 Sales

Covers the complete B2B sales lifecycle from raw prospective lead to closed revenue.

Standard Sales Process Lifecycle:
Lead (Unqualified) → Qualifying Lead → Creates Account + Contact + Opportunity → Quote Generation → Order → Invoice

Pipeline Management

Tracking opportunities by estimated revenue, probability, estimated close date, and visual sales pipeline funnels.

Product Catalog

Defining price lists, unit groups, discount lists, and product bundles for sales quotes and orders.

Sales Copilot & Insights

Generative AI meeting summaries, automated email drafts, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator integration.

🎧 Module 3: Dynamics 365 Customer Service

Case Lifecycle & Queues

  • Cases (Incidents): Central record tracking a customer's inquiry or issue.
  • Queues: Shared holding areas where incoming cases, emails, or activities are assigned and routed.
  • Knowledge Management: Searchable, versioned articles linked to cases for swift first-contact resolution.

Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Entitlements

  • Entitlements: Contractual agreements defining the amount and type of support a customer is eligible to receive (e.g., 50 hours or 20 cases).
  • SLAs: Key performance indicators tracking first response time, resolution time, and warning/escalation actions.

🛠️ Module 4: Dynamics 365 Field Service

Empowers mobile technicians to deliver on-site service with intelligent scheduling and IoT diagnostics.

Work Orders

Defines the on-site service request: location, customer asset, incidents, required parts, skills, and estimated duration.

Schedule Board & URS

Universal Resource Scheduling (URS) dynamically matches work order requirements to technician skills, travel distance, and availability.

Connected Field Service (IoT)

Connects IoT telemetry to Dataverse. Automatically detects equipment anomalies and generates preventive work orders before failure.

💡 Pro-Tips for Exam Success

  • Understand the Lead qualification outcome: Qualifying a Lead in Dynamics 365 Sales creates three records: Account, Contact, and Opportunity.
  • SLAs vs Entitlements: Entitlements specify how much support a customer has purchased; SLAs define how quickly you must respond and resolve.
  • Schedule Board: URS can be used not just for Field Service, but across any Dataverse table requiring resource booking.
